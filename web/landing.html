The map

The map
comes first

You can’t close a gap you can’t see. Every skill in the craft is mapped and made visible, giving you a clear picture of your competencies, blind spots, and areas for growth.

The map includes 71 Product Management skills and 96 System Design skills. Both tracks are open from day one, so professionals can enter from their existing area of expertise and build toward the other side - whether you come from product, engineering, or another discipline.

The red line is the only colour on the map. It stands off the centre in proportion to what you have actually covered.

Lessons

Short, and
never abstract

Every lesson makes the cost of getting the skill wrong explicit and follows one fictional company throughout, giving each concept a consistent real-world context. If you prefer to listen, two hosts discuss the lesson in a concise audio overview.

The exercises between lessons are formative: there’s no score to chase. Instead, you receive the reasoning behind the decision either way, helping you understand not just what the right answer is, but why.
