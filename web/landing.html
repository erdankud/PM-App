The map

The map
comes first

You cannot close a gap you cannot see. Every skill of the craft is one card on one sheet: what it is, the question it answers, the models used in its context.

Seventy-one product skills and ninety-six system-design skills, in the same grammar. Both are open from day one, so somebody arriving from engineering can start on the other side.

The red line is the only colour on the map. It stands off the centre in proportion to what you have actually covered.

Lessons

Short, and
never abstract

Every lesson names the cost of getting the skill wrong, and carries one fictional company through the whole direction. Two hosts talk the lesson over in an audio overview if you would rather listen.

Two hundred and forty-eight of them, in English and Russian. The exercises between them are formative: they carry no score, and the reasoning comes back either way.
